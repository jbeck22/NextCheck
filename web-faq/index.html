NextCheck Web App FAQ

Getting started

What is the NextCheck web app?#

It's NextCheck in your browser. Use the same account as the iPhone and iPad app, and your bills and pay cycles are already there, synced both ways. There's nothing to download.

How do I sign in?#

Use Continue with Google, Continue with Apple, or your email and password. Use the same method you use on your iPhone. New here? Tap Need an account? Sign up.

I forgot my password.#

On the sign-in page, tap Forgot password?, enter your email and tap Send Reset Link. Check your inbox for the link.

Does the web app cost anything?#

No. The web app is free, has no ads, and has no purchases.

Using it

What can I do on the web?#

  • Pay Cycle: see what's left this paycheck, mark bills, and add extra bills and deposits.
  • Bills: add, edit and delete recurring bills.
  • History: past cycles, with total income, total bills and net.
  • Settings: your pay schedule, paychecks, holiday overrides, appearance, and account deletion.

How do I add a bill?#

Go to Bills > + Add Bill. Enter the name, Amount and Due day of month. Optionally add a Pay link, Auto-pay, Split half/half across both checks or Active months (for example, 4-12 for April to December). Tick Keep this open to add another bill to enter several in a row.

How do I mark a bill paid?#

On Pay Cycle, click the bill's status button. It cycles Unpaid → Pending → Cleared. Click Skip to leave a bill out of this cycle, or Include to bring it back.

How do I look at another pay cycle?#

Use the arrows next to the cycle heading on Pay Cycle.

How do I change my paycheck amount for one cycle?#

On Pay Cycle, edit the amount under Amount Available. It defaults to your paycheck from Settings.

How do I set my pay schedule?#

In Settings, set Pay frequency and your paydays, enter your paycheck amounts, then click Save. To handle a holiday, add an override with the Scheduled date and the date it was Actually paid.

How does "Monthly Savings" work on the web?#

Same as the app: name a bill exactly Monthly Savings, and marking it Cleared adds a matching deposit to your savings.

Can I use dark mode?#

Yes. Settings > Appearance: System, Light or Dark.

Can I ask an AI about my budget?#

Yes, it's free for everyone. See Ask your AI in the NextCheck iPhone and iPad FAQ. The connector reads the same account you use on the web.

Privacy

Is the web app private?#

Yes. Your data is locked to your account in Google Firebase and encrypted in transit and at rest. The web app shows no ads. A copy may be cached in your browser's local storage, so sign out on shared computers.

How do I sign out?#

Click Sign Out in the top bar, next to the tabs.

How do I delete my account?#

Go to Settings > Delete Account and confirm. This permanently deletes your bills, cycles, cards and savings. If it says you need a recent sign-in, sign out, sign back in, then try again. This doesn't cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that on your iPhone.

Troubleshooting

I signed in but my bills are missing.#

You're probably signed in with a different method than on your phone, which creates a separate account. Sign out and use the same Google, Apple or email login.

History is empty.#

History fills in as you track pay cycles. If you just changed your pay frequency, cycles saved under your old paydays won't appear.
