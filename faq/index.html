Getting started

What is NextCheck?#

NextCheck is a budgeting app built around your paychecks instead of calendar months. Every payday gets its own plan: which bills it covers, what's cleared, what's pending, and exactly what's left before the next check.

How do I sign up?#

Sign in with Apple, Google or email. Use the same login on iPhone, iPad and the web app, and your data syncs between them.

How do I set up my pay schedule?#

Go to Settings. Under Payday schedule, pick your Pay frequency: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month, or monthly.

  • Twice a month: choose 15th & last day, 1st & 15th, or Custom days.
  • Weekly or every two weeks: set your Anchor payday, which is any real payday.
  • Monthly: set your Payday.

Enter your take-home pay under Paychecks. Changes save automatically.

My payday moved because of a holiday. What do I do?#

In Settings > Payday schedule, add an override. Set Scheduled to the normal date and Actually paid to the date it really paid, then tap Add Override.

What happens if I change my pay frequency?#

NextCheck asks you to confirm. Your pay cycles are recomputed from the new schedule, and past cycles saved under your old paydays won't appear in History.

Everyday use

How do I add a bill?#

Go to the Bills tab and tap Add Bill. Enter the name, amount and due date. Optionally add a Pay link, turn on Auto-pay, turn on Split half/half across both checks, or set Active months for seasonal bills.

What's on the Pay Cycle screen?#

The current paycheck's plan: Available to Spend at the top, how much of your bills are handled, Bills Due This Cycle, Extra Bills This Cycle (one-offs), and Deposits This Cycle.

How do I mark a bill paid?#

Tap the status button on the bill. It cycles Unpaid → Pending → Cleared, then back to Unpaid. Use Pending for payments that are scheduled but haven't cleared yet.

Can I skip a bill for one paycheck?#

Yes. Tap Skip on that bill. It stays in your Bills list, and you can tap Include to bring it back.

What are extra bills and deposits?#

Extra bills are one-offs that aren't part of your recurring list. Deposits are extra money coming in this cycle. Add either with Add on the Pay Cycle screen.

How does "Monthly Savings" work?#

Name a bill exactly Monthly Savings. When you mark it Cleared, NextCheck adds a matching deposit to your Savings tab automatically.

What's in History?#

History shows your past pay cycles. Trends (Pro) shows your whole year of bills, month by month.

NextCheck Pro and purchases

What does NextCheck Pro add?#

Pro unlocks Accounts (credit card tracking and savings projections) and Trends, and removes the banner ad from the free iPhone and iPad app. The core paycheck planner stays free.

How much is Pro?#

$1.99 a month or $14.99 a year in the US. The app shows your local price from the App Store.

How do I upgrade?#

Go to Settings > Upgrade to Pro, or tap Unlock Pro on any Pro screen. Choose Monthly or Annual and tap Continue.

I already paid. How do I restore Pro?#

Open the Pro screen (Settings > Upgrade to Pro) and tap Restore Purchases. If you see "No previous purchases found for this Apple Account", check that you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you paid with. Pro is also tied to your NextCheck account, so it carries over when you sign in on another device.

Does Pro work on my iPad too?#

Yes. Sign in to NextCheck on your iPad with the same account.

How do I cancel?#

Cancel in your iPhone or iPad Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > NextCheck. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your NextCheck account doesn't cancel the subscription.

Privacy and security

Does NextCheck connect to my bank?#

No. NextCheck has no bank connections. Everything in it is what you typed in yourself.

Who can see my data?#

Only you. Your data is stored in Google Firebase, locked to your account and encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share it. The free iPhone and iPad app shows a non-personalized Google AdMob banner, which Pro removes. The web app has no ads.

Can I lock the app?#

Yes. Turn on Settings > Security > Require Face ID / passcode (or Touch ID). NextCheck then locks whenever you leave it.

How do I delete my account?#

Go to Settings > Delete Account and confirm with Delete Everything. This permanently erases your bills, cycles, cards and savings, and can't be undone. If NextCheck asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in, and try again.

Troubleshooting

My bills aren't showing on another device.#

Make sure you're signed in with the same account (Apple, Google or email) on both. If you used Apple on your iPhone, use Continue with Apple on the web too.

"Subscriptions aren't available right now."#

The App Store couldn't load the plans. Check your connection and try again later.

"App Lock Unavailable"#

Your device has no Face ID, Touch ID or passcode set up. Set one up in iPhone Settings, then turn the lock on again.

Ask your AI (NextCheck connector)

Connect NextCheck to an AI assistant such as Claude. It is free for every NextCheck user and read-only.

What it is

What is "Ask your AI"?#

It's a free connector that lets an AI assistant, like Claude, read your NextCheck bills and pay cycles so you can ask questions in plain words, such as "What's due this week?" or "How much is left after this paycheck?". It's built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and the connector address is https://mcp.nextcheck.net/mcp.

Do I need NextCheck Pro?#

No. It's free for every NextCheck user.

Setting it up

Which NextCheck account should I sign in with?#

The same one you use in the app. The sign-in page shows "Signed in as …" before you allow access. If it's the wrong account, don't approve; go back and sign in again.

What does the "This app registered itself; its name isn't verified" note mean?#

The sign-in page shows who's asking for access. If the app didn't publish a verified name, NextCheck tells you. Only continue if you just started connecting from your AI app. If the page warns that access goes to an app on your computer, continue only if you started the connection from that app.

What it can and can't do

What can my AI see?#

Two read-only tools:

  • List bills (list_bills): your recurring bills, with name, amount and due day, plus auto-pay, split-across-paychecks, active months and notes when set.
  • Pay cycle summary (get_cycle_summary): for the current pay cycle, or any date you name, it gives the payday and next payday, your paycheck amount, each bill due with its status (cleared, pending, unpaid or skipped), one-off bills, extra deposits, and totals: bills due, cleared, pending, still owed and left after bills.

Can my AI change anything?#

No. It can't add, change, mark paid or delete anything. To change your budget, use the NextCheck app or the web app.

Can it see my credit cards, savings or trends?#

No. Right now it only reads bills and pay cycles.

Can it look at a past or future pay cycle?#

Yes. Name a date, like "the paycheck on December 5", and it looks up the pay cycle that contains that date.

Does it work with any pay schedule?#

Yes: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly, including your holiday overrides. Set up your pay schedule in the app first.

Privacy and security

What data is shared with the AI?#

Only the bill and pay cycle details listed above, and only when your assistant looks something up to answer you. What the AI provider does with those answers is covered by its own privacy policy.

Does NextCheck store a copy of my data on the connector?#

No. The connector keeps only an encrypted sign-in token, on Cloudflare, so you stay connected. It reads your account fresh each time, and it can only read your own account.

Does my AI get my password?#

No. You sign in on NextCheck's own page, and the AI app only gets read-only access.

How do I disconnect?#

Remove or disconnect NextCheck in your AI app's connector settings (in Claude: Settings > Connectors). Deleting your NextCheck account also ends access.

Troubleshooting

The sign-in buttons don't do anything.#

Google and Apple sign-in open a pop-up. Allow pop-ups for mcp.nextcheck.net, or sign in with email.

I see "This page expired".#

The connection request timed out or was opened in another browser. Start connecting again from your AI app.

I see "Sign-in failed" or "Account mismatch".#

Go back and sign in again with the same account. If it keeps happening, remove the connector and add it again.

My AI says "NextCheck sign-in expired or was revoked".#

Disconnect NextCheck in your AI app, then connect and sign in again.

My AI says my pay schedule isn't set up.#

Open NextCheck, go to Settings, and set your pay frequency and payday. Then ask again.

It shows tomorrow's pay cycle late in the evening.#

Tell your assistant today's date, for example "Today is November 13". Without a date, the connector uses UTC, which is already tomorrow on US evenings.

The numbers don't match the app.#

The connector uses the same math as the NextCheck web app. Make sure you're asking about the same pay cycle, and that the app finished syncing your latest changes.
