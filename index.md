# NextCheck Support

NextCheck is a personal budgeting app for iPhone and iPad that plans your bills around your actual pay schedule — paycheck by paycheck, not month by month.

## Get help

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: **jeramey.beck@gmail.com**

We aim to respond within a few business days.

## Privacy

Read our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).

You can permanently delete your account and all data at any time from **Settings → Delete Account** inside the app.
