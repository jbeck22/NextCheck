Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

NextCheck ("the app") is a personal budgeting app operated by Jeramey Beck ("we," "us"), available for iPhone and iPad and as a web app you can use in your browser. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the name and email address those providers share with us. We never see your Google or Apple password.

Financial data you enter. NextCheck stores the budgeting information you type into the app: bills, paycheck amounts, pay schedules, credit card charges and payments, savings entries, and related notes. NextCheck does not connect to your bank. It has no access to your bank accounts, card networks, or credit reports — everything in the app is data you entered yourself.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to NextCheck Pro, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We receive subscription status (active/expired) but never your payment details.

Advertising (free tier, iOS app only). The free tier of the iPhone/iPad app shows a small banner ad to help keep the core paycheck planner free. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are non-personalized: we do not use advertising identifiers for tracking, and we do not link ad data to your NextCheck account. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage or diagnostic data to serve and measure these ads, per Google's Privacy Policy. Upgrading to NextCheck Pro removes ads entirely. The web app shows no ads.

What we don't collect

  • No bank or financial institution connections — ever
  • No sale of your data, to anyone, for any reason
  • No contacts, photos, or other device content
  • No behavioral tracking for advertising purposes

How your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), secured so that each account can only access its own data. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest on Google's infrastructure. A local copy may be cached on your device, protected by iOS device encryption (or your browser's local storage on the web). If you enable the app-lock feature, opening the app additionally requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode; biometric data never leaves your device and is never visible to us.

How your data is used

Solely to provide the app's functionality: syncing your budget between your devices and showing it back to you. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than operating NextCheck.

Service providers

We rely on these processors to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database hosting)
  • Apple (sign-in with Apple, subscription billing)
  • Google (sign-in with Google)
  • Google AdMob (non-personalized banner ads on the iOS free tier; see Advertising above)
  • RevenueCat (subscription status management; receives anonymous purchase receipts, never your financial entries)

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This erases your data from our servers immediately and cannot be undone. You may also email us (below) to request deletion.

Children

NextCheck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app's release notes. (October 2026: added the Advertising section to describe the non-personalized ad banner introduced in v1.1.0.)
